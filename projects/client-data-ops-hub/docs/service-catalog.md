# Service Catalogue

This project presents a small, productized data operations service for companies that need reliable reporting without building every capability in-house.

## Core offer

**Managed KPI delivery:** receive agreed exports, validate them, transform them and deliver a recurring scorecard with quality and SLA evidence.

## Engagement modules

| Module | Client problem | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Data intake | Files arrive differently every cycle | Source inventory and intake checklist |
| Data foundation | Metrics disagree across teams | Data contract, typed models and metric definitions |
| KPI delivery | Leaders wait for manual reports | Executive scorecard and operational tables |
| Quality gate | Errors are discovered in meetings | Automated checks, incident log and delivery status |
| Ongoing operations | No one owns recurring data work | Runbook, SLA report and review cadence |

## Example monthly delivery

1. Client uploads four agreed exports.
2. Partner confirms receipt and schema compatibility.
3. Pipeline runs the quality gate.
4. Failed critical checks block publication and create an incident.
5. Successful checks produce the scorecard, account health and daily operations tables.
6. Partner shares the manifest, business summary and open questions.
7. Client reviews the output and accepts the delivery.

## What the client receives

- Decision-ready tables, not raw data dumps
- Definitions for every important metric
- A visible quality status
- A clear owner and recovery path when the delivery fails
- A repeatable process that can be expanded to new sources

This is a proposal framework, not a pricing sheet. Scope and pricing should be agreed after discovery, based on source count, frequency, SLA, privacy requirements and reporting complexity.
